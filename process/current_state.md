# Current-State Process Analysis

## Process Objective

Identify products requiring replenishment, communicate the need, complete the replenishment activity, and communicate completion.

## Current-State Workflow

1. **Inventory need identified** through physical observation, system lookup, customer request, supervisor direction, or routine department walks.
2. **Inventory information checked** using available system information.
3. **Replenishment need communicated** verbally, by radio, written note, department tool, or supervisor communication.
4. **Supervisor reviews need** and determines priority based on demand, customer impact, workload, and other considerations.
5. **Associate performs replenishment** while balancing customer service and other operational duties.
6. **Completion communicated** through an available communication method.
7. **Supervisor verifies** completion during a department walk or follow-up.

## Current-State Flow

```text
Need Identified
      ↓
Inventory Checked
      ↓
Replenishment Needed?
   ↙          ↘
 No            Yes
 ↓              ↓
Continue    Communicate Need
Operations       ↓
           Supervisor Review
                  ↓
             Prioritize
                  ↓
          Perform Replenishment
                  ↓
          Communicate Completion
                  ↓
            Supervisor Verify
```

## Pain Points

| Area | Pain Point | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Identification | Relies partly on manual observation | Needs may be identified inconsistently |
| Communication | Multiple communication methods | Information can be missed or duplicated |
| Prioritization | May vary by person or shift | Important work may not be addressed first |
| Assignment | Limited centralized ownership | Accountability is unclear |
| Tracking | Limited status visibility | Managers may not know what remains |
| Completion | May be informal | Difficult to measure performance |
| Reporting | Limited historical data | Recurring issues are difficult to analyze |
| Labor | Time spent identifying/communicating needs | Less time for execution/customer service |

## Key BSA Observation

The problem is not simply that associates need to replenish products.

The larger process problem is the absence of a consistent workflow for **identifying, prioritizing, assigning, tracking, and measuring replenishment work**.

## Improvement Opportunities

- Standardize replenishment criteria.
- Create a centralized task queue.
- Establish priority levels.
- Assign ownership.
- Track task status.
- Record completion time.
- Escalate overdue work.
- Capture exception reasons.
- Provide management reporting.
